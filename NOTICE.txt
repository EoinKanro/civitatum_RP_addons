Civitatum Races expands TeaCraft PlayerAppearance 0.1.0 (MIT).
Its renderer uses the original PlayerAppearance ModelPart layer structure.
No Dragon Survival code, textures, models or GUI assets are included.
Female Gender Mod 3.2.2 settings were inspected from its public release;
the optional adapter invokes its public methods without embedding its code.
Reference: https://github.com/FemaleGenderMod/FemaleGenderMod
Race concept art and author lore are provided by the project author.
Race-selection portraits are newly generated pixel-style illustrations.
